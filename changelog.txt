---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.6.0
Date: 21. 11. 2026
  Changes:
    - Repair progress is shown in a panel beside the wreck's window, with a progress bar, in place of text above the wreck.
    - The systems stage is now 20 steps of 10 processing units, 1 rocket control unit, 1 low density structure and 1 glass.
    - Repair steps no longer produce a hidden item; the wreck counts its own finished crafts.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.5.0
Date: 21. 11. 2026
  Changes:
    - Repair stages are now done in steps: 100 for the hull, 25 for systems and 20 for propulsion. The wreck's recipe is one step.
    - Progress is shown as a percentage above the wreck.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.4.0
Date: 20. 11. 2026
  Changes:
    - Crude recipes now cost about twice the raw materials of Space Exploration's own recipes.
    - Crude recipes and technologies are named "Crude spaceship floor" and so on.
    - Only the first two spaceship integrity levels move earlier; the rest stay where Space Exploration puts them.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.0
Date: 20. 11. 2026
  Changes:
    - Space Exploration's spaceship part recipes and their technologies are no longer changed.
    - The early recipes are now separate Crude recipes, unlocked by new Crude technologies at rocket science. The Refined recipes and technologies are gone.
    - Repair costs are based on the crude recipes.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 20. 11. 2026
  Features:
    - Spaceships now move to rocket science in this mod itself; Early Spaceships is no longer needed and is marked incompatible.
    - Every spaceship part has an early recipe and a refined recipe. The refined ones are Space Exploration's originals, unlocked by new Refined technologies.
  Changes:
    - The repaired ship has a tapered nose, 12 x 21 tiles overall, and is fully streamlined.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.1
Date: 20. 11. 2026
  Changes:
    - The repaired ship is larger, 12 x 19 tiles with two engines and two booster tanks, to match the size of the wreck.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 20. 11. 2026
  Features:
    - The crashed spaceship becomes a repair site with three stages: hull, systems and propulsion.
    - Finishing the last stage rebuilds the wreck as a working Space Exploration spaceship.
    - Added the Spaceship repair technology, which the Spaceship technology now follows.
